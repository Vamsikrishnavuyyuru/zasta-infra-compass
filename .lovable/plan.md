# New Zasta Corporate Presentation

## Deliverable
Create a completely new professional Microsoft PowerPoint presentation in legacy **`.ppt` format only**. The website, its pages, code, and functionality will remain unchanged.

## Creative Direction
- Use a fresh editorial corporate style that is clearly different from the previous deck.
- Favor white and very light neutral backgrounds with restrained Zasta green, graphite, and muted gold accents.
- Preserve the supplied Zasta logo exactly, at its original proportions, without redrawing, stretching, recoloring, or reshaping it.
- Use concise wording, strong hierarchy, clean diagrams, and generous whitespace.
- Create a new, cohesive AI-generated visual for every slide; no image will be reused, and none of the previous presentation images will be used.
- Keep generated images free of logos and baked-in text so facts and branding remain editable and accurate.

## Slide Story
Approximately **9 slides** in 16:9 format:

1. **Cover** — Zasta Enterprises Private Limited, “Excellence Always,” Hyderabad positioning, original logo, and a new infrastructure image.
2. **Company Profile** — concise overview, global infrastructure focus, and key scale indicators: 170+ expert engineers, 40+ completed projects, and 30+ current projects.
3. **Integrated Capabilities** — workforce solutions, construction project management, engineering consultancy, and digital platforms, supported by a new engineering-delivery image.
4. **Sectors We Serve** — power and distribution, transportation, oil and gas, IT/data centers, real estate, and industries/warehousing, with a new multi-sector visual.
5. **CONQUAL Overview** — presentation-only naming of the Construction Quality Management Platform, its purpose, and core field-quality workflow, with a new site-quality technology image.
6. **CONQUAL Capabilities** — 2,000+ checklists, offline working, real-time audits, photo/GPS records, AI reporting, and cloud synchronization, with a different new inspection visual.
7. **NirmanJobs** — AI-powered construction hiring, intelligent matching, smart JD parsing, verified engineer database, faster hiring, interview scheduling, and `nirmanjobs.com`, with a new recruitment image.
8. **Why Zasta + Trust** — agility, quality, domain knowledge, rapid response, D-U-N-S® 77-141-6338, ISO 9001:2015, DPIIT Startup India, and Udyam/MSME registration, with a new trust/engineering image.
9. **Contact / Closing** — Hyderabad office, phone, email, website, and a clear partnership message, with a new future-infrastructure image.

## Content Rules
- Use the current website as the source of truth for company facts, services, sectors, credentials, and contact details.
- Mention **CONQUAL only in this presentation**, as explicitly requested; do not add it back to the website.
- Do not invent customers, revenue, financial data, project names, testimonials, or performance claims.
- Keep explanations brief but complete, especially for Zasta, CONQUAL, and NirmanJobs.

## Quality Assurance
- Generate and place nine distinct images, one per slide, with no duplication from the prior deck.
- Convert the finished presentation to genuine legacy `.ppt` format and verify the file type.
- Render every slide for visual inspection, checking clipping, overlap, text fit, image clarity, logo proportions, and consistent styling.
- Perform at least one correction and re-verification pass.
- Deliver only the final `.ppt` file with a concise QA note.
